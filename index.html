          ARCHITECTURE

          Four steps between the scream and the siren

          01

          Hear

          Browser speech capture or raw transcript — no app, no account, no GPS. The caller speaks however they speak.

          02

          Understand

          Gemini 2.5 Flash runs one engineered triage prompt: language ID → normalisation → severity rubric → landmark geolocation → hazard extraction.

          03

          Structure

          Strict JSON schema output. No free text, no hallucinated coordinates — every field is grounded in the transcript or marked UNKNOWN.

          04

          Act

          Card renders for the operator, first aid reads aloud in the caller's own language, handoff copies straight into dispatch.
